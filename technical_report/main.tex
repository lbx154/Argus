\begin{abstractbox}
\small
\textbf{Abstract.}
An agent harness lets a model reach the real world. Something still has to decide
where to reach, when the result is good enough, and when to stop and ask. That seat
has always held a person, which bounds agentic progress by a resource nobody can
scale: human waking hours. We call its occupant the \emph{Driver} and hand it to the
machine. Its three questions---is this done, what is worth doing next, does this need
a person---are trivial under a dense reward and hard without one, which is why
self-improving agents have advanced fastest where scores exist and why that progress
has not transferred. \systemname{} answers all three without one. The result is
unattended multi-day research: campaigns that run for days at a time with nobody in
the Driver's seat, under \emph{evidence-driven} rather than goal-driven control---the
next step follows from what the campaign has established, not from what the plan
asserted before any of it was known.

Each question goes to a different party. The first is answered by an independent
read-only Reviewer that cannot edit the evidence it judges and may withhold
completion, the second by Manager-owned stage authority over a durable campaign, and
the third by a boundary that stops for credentials, money, and irreversible actions
while absorbing ordinary technical failure. Verification is therefore not a quality
filter bolted onto execution; it is what makes the seat delegable at all. Where no
score exists the runtime does not manufacture one; that would have the same system
produce both the work and its grade. It records progress as a typed transition in
which informative failure still counts, refuses to let an experiment that never ran
refute a hypothesis, and holds the objective as revisable---if the objective is
wrong, departing from it is correct. Domain competence enters through the same seam:
a \emph{vertical} declares what counts as evidence in a field---stage order,
checklists, completion gates---while the \VerticalCoreLoc{}-line core that decides
who may certify and when to ask a person stays fixed. \VerticalCount{} ship today,
the smallest \VerticalSmallest{} lines, and none of them can widen its own authority.

We measure the delegation. Across \DutyCampaigns{} campaigns spanning
\DutyWallHours{} wall-clock hours, \systemname{} asked for a human
\DutyInterruptions{} times---once every \DutyInterval{} hours. The rest of the time
it was working rather than waiting: given work in hand, it was computing for
\DutyCycleLow--\DutyCycleHigh\% of the time available to it. The one campaign that
fell to \DutyDegradedCycle\% is the one whose GPU driver failed, which is where the
real ceiling sits---broken infrastructure, not an agent running out of things to do.
A turn-by-turn coding agent sustains roughly \CodexEndurance{} hour unattended by
comparison.

Those hours produced work, most stringently where the verifier is unforgiving.
\systemname{} carried six diffusion-LLM mask families into a FlashAttention-4 kernel
across two GPU architectures, passing all \FlashDAParity{} parity cases within
\FlashDAGapLow--\FlashDAGapHigh\% of native for under \FlashDACostCNY{} CNY of model
spend, after abandoning a first route that ran slower than the baseline and keeping
it as evidence. It finished an SGLang integration that one engineer had not completed
in over \SGLangHumanHours{} hours, and \FLAPRTotal{} kernels it authored were
accepted by outside maintainers of \code{flash-linear-attention} with no blocking
changes. Judged by an external chemistry validator, it raised MOF structural
discrimination from 0.594 to 0.833 AUC and showed a \emph{simpler} sampler beating
the published one at matched compute. Across seven benchmark arenas the same runtime reaches
approximately \SWEProArgusAccuracy\% on SWE-Bench Pro against
\SWEProDirectAccuracy\% for direct Copilot, and declares \ReviewerFirstBlocked{}
tasks blocked rather than reporting an unsupported success.

The unattended hours leave something behind besides the work. Multi-day autonomy is
what an AI Driver reaches first, ahead of the models it drives, and the runtime
records reaching it at length:
\CampaignsOverWeek{} campaigns beyond a week, the longest \LongestCampaignDays{}
days, one trace of \LargestTraceEvents{} events, each a typed record of a premise
adopted early, contradicted days later, and revised with every intermediate verdict
retained. Scraped corpora contain the artifacts of such work and almost none of the
deliberation, because nobody was ever paid to write down the reasoning that failed.
Pointed inward, \systemname{} has also built the from-scratch 1B pretraining stack
such traces would feed. Today that judgment is supplied by scaffolding. Whether it can instead
be trained into the weights is the question these traces exist to answer.
\end{abstractbox}

  diagram shows how the three Driver questions are distributed. The Reviewer answers
  Q1 and runs read-only, so it cannot edit the evidence it judges; its four verdicts
  include \code{blocked}, which withholds completion outright. The Planner answers Q2
  under a Manager that holds sole authority over Stage transitions. Q3 is an explicit
  boundary: credentials, budget, publication, and irreversible actions stop for the
  operator, while ordinary technical failure does not. The dashed return path exists because a
  Reviewer sees evidence the Planner did not have: holding a result that contradicts

  work done under it, and the Manager decides whether to keep, revise, or replace it,
  which prevents an early decision from binding better-informed rounds

  whose knowledge, event log, artifacts, backlog, budget, daemon, and memory persist
  across bounded missions. The cards immediately
  surrounding it report task-native benchmark outcomes together with the two
  measurements of unattended operation: how long the runtime goes before it needs a
  person, and what share of its available time it spends producing. The
  human-driven comparison on the second card is arithmetic rather than a measured
  system: a harness cannot outlast its operator's working day, which bounds it near
  a third of wall-clock time however capable its model becomes. Bars use independent scales and indicate comparison direction with
  arrows; they are breadth evidence rather than a single normalized leaderboard. The
  lower band summarizes delivered work in three domains where the deciding evaluator
  is external to the runtime: a compiler and a reference implementation for
  infrastructure, an independent chemistry validator and a proof checker for science,
  and a static-timing engine for silicon. The silicon card also records the human
  cost of that campaign---eight days, six interruptions, two of them caused by the
  server failing rather than by the runtime asking.}
