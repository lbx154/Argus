\section{Roadmap}
\label{sec:roadmap}

The roadmap follows directly from the limitations in
Section~\ref{sec:limitations}. Each item is an engineering objective, not a
capability promise, and each is stated so that its completion is checkable.

\subsection{From snapshot to reproducible evidence}

The highest-priority item is to convert the four website-snapshot results of
Table~\ref{tab:results} --- SOL-ExecBench, nanochat H100, AARRI-Bench, and Arbor ---
into locally corroborated artifacts tied to a named commit, matching the nanoGPT
and nanochat-B200 rows that already carry corroboration. The target is that every
published number can make a specific, auditable claim on stated hardware,
verifiable by a third party from a commit and an artifact digest.

\subsection{Strengthening measurement integrity}

Integrity coverage grows per benchmark: the plan is to extend the anti-cheat
construction as each new arena reveals its exploit surface, and to grow the
structured stage checklists the Reviewer verifies against --- rather than
re-introducing a separate family of machine validators that could silently disagree
with the completion authority. The constraint is to keep exactly one owner of
completion (Section~\ref{sec:evidence}) while widening what it is equipped to check.

\subsection{Reducing the single-authority risk}

The sole-reviewer concentration (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}) is the most
important open problem. Near-term work reduces Reviewer error through richer
execution-log auditing and evidence-grounded verdicts, and hardens detection of a
degraded reviewer backend so a blind gate cannot recur. Whether to add independent
cross-checks without recreating a hardcoded gate is an open design question rather
than a settled plan.

\subsection{Retiring debt and lowering cost}

The back-compatibility surface (legacy shims, the retained JSON checkpoint field,
legacy queue helpers) is scheduled for removal once migration paths are confirmed,
and the GPU lease is a candidate for tighter but still explicit scheduler
coordination. In parallel, tightening budget accounting and the cockpit --- better
cost attribution, clearer paused/blocked surfacing, lower-overhead observation ---
makes a 7$\times$24 campaign cheaper to run and easier to steer.

\begin{callout}[The through-line]
Argus today is a research infrastructure for reliable, auditable autonomy at
version 0.2. The roadmap is the path from a trustworthy runtime to trustworthy
\emph{results} --- reproducible evidence, broader integrity coverage, a less
concentrated completion authority, and a cheaper campaign --- pursued without
letting the infrastructure substitute its judgment for the agent's, or any number
outrun its evidence.
\end{callout}
