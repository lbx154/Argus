\section{Key Interfaces and Status Taxonomy}
\label{app:interfaces}\label{app:status}

The role decision interfaces are summarized in Section~\ref{sec:roles}
(Tables~\ref{tab:manager}--\ref{tab:reviewer}). This appendix records the status
taxonomy they resolve into. Three enumerations govern outcomes: \code{ReviewStatus}
(the verdict), \code{LoopStatus} (a mission's terminal or paused outcome), and
\code{PlannerVerdictStatus} (the planner's event contract), the last carrying an
explicit (success, recoverable) policy that determines how the scheduler reacts.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{4.2cm} c c X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{PlannerVerdictStatus} & \textbf{success} & \textbf{recoverable} & \textbf{Meaning} \\
\midrule
\code{planned}                   & yes & no  & a new task batch was produced \\
\code{completed}                 & yes & no  & the project is certified complete \\
\code{research\_incomplete}      & no  & yes & more research is required \\
\code{paused\_budget}            & no  & yes & budget exhausted; recheck later \\
\code{paused\_no\_breakthrough}  & no  & yes & no breakthrough this cycle \\
\code{exhausted\_current\_methods} & no & yes & current methods exhausted \\

\noindent\textbf{ReviewStatus} (\code{core/models.py}):
\code{done}, \code{continue}, \code{blocked}, plus the research-pause states
\code{research\_incomplete}, \code{paused\_no\_breakthrough}, and
\code{exhausted\_current\_methods}.

\noindent\textbf{LoopStatus} (\code{core/models.py}): the terminal set
\code{done}, \code{max\_rounds}, \code{blocked}, \code{no\_progress},
\code{error}, \code{budget\_exhausted}; and the paused set \code{paused\_budget},
\code{paused\_provider\_cooldown}, \code{paused\_provider\_fence},
\code{infra\_blocked}, \code{research\_incomplete}, \code{paused\_no\_breakthrough},
\code{exhausted\_current\_methods}; and the control-transfer status
\code{replan\_requested}, used only when dynamic planning requests a return to the
Planner. Every mission yields one of these statuses --- never an unbounded spin
(Section~\ref{sec:runtime}).

Table~\ref{tab:persist} (Section~\ref{sec:state}) lists the principal state files;

cross-project identity, the shared skill library, and an operator knob store; each
\code{projects/<fingerprint>/} subtree holds the append-only \code{events.jsonl},
\code{backlog.jsonl}, \code{continuous.json}, \code{inbox.jsonl}, a \code{skills/}
directory, \code{daemon.pid}, \code{daemon.status.json}, and
\code{checkpoint.json}.
A runtime path resolver rejects unresolved shell placeholders, and the project-root
helper rejects empty, \code{.}, and \code{/} fingerprints, so a mis-resolved path
fails loudly rather than writing to the wrong place.
