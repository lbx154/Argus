\section{The Process Is the Retained Asset}
\label{sec:process}\label{sec:processdata}

\begin{designbox}
Keep more than the answer. A completed mission yields a final artifact, but the
durable value is the recorded process that produced it --- the states, actions,
evidence, and review feedback, together with what \emph{changed} in the runtime state
and which branches failed. Persist that process as typed, credential-redacted
records, never as raw private model reasoning.
\end{designbox}

The evidence plane already records every mission as an append-only timeline
(Section~\ref{sec:evidence}). Read as data, that timeline is strictly richer than the
deliverable. Let $D_{\mathrm{final}}$ be the final artifact of a mission and
$D_{\mathrm{process}}$ the recorded run that produced it.

\begin{equation}
D_{\mathrm{process}}
= \{(s_k,a_k,e_k,r_k,\Delta H_k)\}_{k=1}^{N}
\supsetneq
D_{\mathrm{final}}=\{y^\star\}.
\end{equation}

The strict containment is the point. The process record keeps the full per-round
tuple --- states, actions, evidence, feedback, and runtime-state deltas --- not only
the artifact $y^\star$. In each round $k$: $s_k$ is the round \emph{state} (goal,
open blocker, and next step in the curated \code{checkpoint.json}); $a_k$ is the
\emph{action} taken (tool and provider calls, each correlated to the tape by
\code{call\_id}); $e_k$ is the \emph{evidence} produced (artifacts, measurements, and
provenance digests); $r_k$ is the Reviewer's \emph{feedback} (the verdict, its
\code{progress\_class}, and the next action); and $\Delta H_k$ is the
\emph{runtime-state delta} from Section~\ref{sec:evolution} --- a skill version bump,
a wiki source, or a checkpoint rewrite. Crucially, $D_{\mathrm{process}}$ retains the
branches that did \emph{not} reach $y^\star$: dead ends recorded in the checkpoint's
tried-and-failed list, and counterexamples and \textsc{no-go} findings written to the
journal and wiki.

\subsection{Where each part is stored}

Each element lands on a mechanism already built. The typed
\code{events.jsonl} tape holds the ordered states, actions, evidence, and verdicts;
the curated \code{checkpoint.json} holds the current-state summary and the bounded
tried-and-failed list; versioned \code{skills/} carry the reusable procedures learned
along the way, with their history as an audit of what changed; and the per-project
\code{wiki/} records an immutable source card after each verdict, from which the
Reviewer may synthesize an evidence-cited page (Sections~\ref{sec:lifecycle},
\ref{sec:evidence}). Together these make $D_{\mathrm{process}}$ replayable rather than
anecdotal.

One boundary is deliberate. What persists is the typed, credential-redacted event
record --- artifacts, measurements, tool calls, and verdicts --- \emph{not} the
model's raw private reasoning transcript, and this report makes no claim that such
traces are published. The secret guard scrubs credentials before anything reaches the
tape or the Reviewer, and provenance carries no local absolute paths, usernames, or
session ids (Section~\ref{sec:evidence}). The retained process is an auditable public
record of \emph{what was done and shown}, held to the same redaction stance as every
other persisted artifact.
