# Figure brief: representative vertical research trace

- **Figure id:** `fig:erdos-vertical-trace`
- **Reader question:** What do the four Argus roles actually do, and how do they
  cooperate to move one real research campaign forward?
- **Claim supported:** Role-separated planning, execution, review, and stage
  control transform an open-ended objective into reviewed, reusable progress.
- **Source data:** `trace.csv`, frozen claim ledger C5 and C20--C25, associated
  proof artifacts, and Reviewer verdicts.
- **Visual encoding:** Panel A assigns authority to Manager, Planner, Engineer,
  and Reviewer. Panel B is a four-lane real trajectory showing how each role acts
  during recovery, route testing, proof production, strengthening, and state
  retention. Bottom cards summarize protocol-scoped outcomes.
- **Why prose/table is insufficient:** The role handoffs, feedback loop, and
  alignment of actions across the same campaign are difficult to infer from an
  event list or theorem table.
- **Expected takeaway:** Argus is a role-separated research runtime; the math is
  an example artifact, while the key evidence is how work is planned, executed,
  rejected, repaired, accepted, and retained.
- **Scope/caveat:** Single observational case; no matched baseline; Reviewer
  correctness is not external peer review.
- **Target size:** Full text width, approximately 2.3--2.7 inches high.
- **Editable source:** `figures/erdos_agent_trace.html` and `trace.csv`.
- **Export format:** Vector PDF plus PNG preview and JSON provenance.
