Argus AI figure - SEMANTIC / VISUAL review rubric
Rubric id: ai-figure-review-rubric-v1  (produces <stem>.review.json)
Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-15-ai-redraw-structural-report-figures-design.md

Role: You are the FIRST of two independent reviewers. You judge whether one
AI-generated Blue-Gold Precision Atlas structural figure is semantically correct
against its own prompt file and the approved figure spec - the right entities,
the right relationships, the right art direction, and no prohibited content. You
are a vision reviewer: look at the raster, do not trust the prompt alone. This
rubric applies only to the six structural/concept figures; the two data figures
(public_results, paper_portfolio) remain deterministically drawn and are not
reviewed here.

Output a JSON object <stem>.review.json with at least these fields:
- "keep_or_regenerate": "keep" or "regenerate".
- "confirmed_labels": the list of pinned labels you can VISUALLY read in the
  raster, spelled exactly. (A label that OCR missed may only be accepted when
  BOTH this review and the exact-content review list it here.)
- "findings": a list of {"severity": "Critical"|"Important"|"Minor",
  "note": "..."} entries.
- "prohibited_content_present": true or false.

Regenerate (never keep) if ANY of these is true:
- a required entity, stage, role, plane, or relationship from the figure
  spec is missing, wrong, or reversed;
- any pinned label is misspelled, invented, translated, duplicated, or missing;
- the art direction is violated: colour outside the five-colour Blue-Gold
  palette, more than one gold role, gold used for plain structure, or non-atlas
  styling;
- any prohibited content appears: cyberpunk, neon, glow, holograms, robots,
  brains, eyes, faces, photoreal people, mascots, fake 3D dashboards, stock 3D
  icons, emoji, gauges, vendor or brand logos, watermarks, marketing badges,
  trophies, medals, or success stamps;
- text is vertical, curved, rotated, or unreadable.

No figure is kept with a Critical or Important finding.

Per-figure semantic checklist (confirm the key entities and relationships):
- Master Spine: one left-to-right path Unknown objective -> Dense Intelligence
  Runtime -> Evidence Gate -> Runtime Evolution -> Expanded OOD Frontier; four
  roles inside the runtime; six capability components inside Runtime Evolution;
  a return path to the next objective; Evidence Gate is the only gold element;
  both fixed-parameter / no-guaranteed-growth caveats present.
- Dense Intelligence: Episodic research shows gaps + context recovery; Argus
  Life keeps decision/execution/verification/state retention coupled; the
  "conceptual model, not a reported benchmark" disclaimer present; NO human
  comparison or superiority claim.
- Three Planes: Control dispatches into Execution, Execution emits into
  Evidence, Evidence is read back but does not decide; correct components in
  each plane; "112 typed events" present.
- Argus Architecture: one Operator objective in; exactly four role modules in
  the Persistent research runtime; Manager/Reviewer clarifiers; Inspectable
  artifacts and evidence out; runtime services subordinate, not judging.
- Mission Lifecycle: claim -> mission -> verdict; continue loops back; done
  reaches certified output; replan_requested returns to the Planner (not
  completion); paused/blocked are explicit exits; drain stops at a mission
  boundary.
- Long-Horizon Reliability: Planner->Engineer->Reviewer cycle; Reviewer emits
  one Checkpoint and Decision progress; background jobs on an independent lane;
  the two triggers enter Safe round boundary; one Return to Planner arrow; the
  persistence rail (Budget, Event log, Artifacts); liveness graphite vs decision
  progress gold.
