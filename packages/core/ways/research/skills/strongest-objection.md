# The strongest objection

Use this when reviewing a paper, to find the one scientific objection that would sink it at
the venue, or when reviewing an idea route, to tell a reason to set the route aside from a
risk for Experiment.

## On a paper

Read the current paper and identify the single strongest venue-level rejection argument that
survives the obvious rebuttal. Put in your letter:

1. the decisive objection;
2. the exact section, figure, table, or result supporting it;
3. the strongest available defense;
4. the remaining defect that would justify rejection at the venue, and the direct repair.

Do not write the objection and its answer into separate files. The Engineer repairs the issue
together with the other findings, and the Reviewer then judges whether the repaired paper
holds.

## On an idea route

State the one strongest objection to the route as written and its best defense in a few
sentences; do not redo the literature search. The objection sets the route aside only when the
same mechanism and claim are already published, the mechanism is contradicted by evidence, or
the decisive experiment needs resources not here. Anything else, including partial overlap
with prior work, is a known risk: record it for Experiment and let the route pass.
