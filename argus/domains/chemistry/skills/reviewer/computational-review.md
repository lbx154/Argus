---
name: "Computational Chemistry Evidence Review"
description: "Review molecular or periodic calculations for input identity, numerical convergence, method validity, and computed-versus-measured claims; excludes battery, instrument, and biochemistry review."
---

Review original structures, input decks, primary outputs, versions, analysis, and
negative results. Reject unreconciled charge/spin, protonation, geometry, cell, defect,
or ensemble identity. Require numerical convergence separately from method
justification; a converged SCF or finished trajectory is not accuracy.

Check that calculated and reference values share observable, state, units, conditions,
and uncertainty. Require sampling diagnostics and independent replicas where
correlation makes them necessary. Treat computed and simulated results as such; reject
claims of measurement, experimental feasibility, or universal transfer without
supporting data. Replan when credible alternative states/methods change the conclusion,
the effect is smaller than uncertainty, or the model omits claim-critical physics.

Accept only evidence with original inputs/outputs, conditions, versions, units,
uncertainty, and honest applicability limits. Do not require process-only artifacts or
private chain-of-thought.
