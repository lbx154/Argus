---
name: "Materials Processing Structure Property Validation"
description: "Validate materials processing-structure-property claims with comparable specimens, controls, uncertainty, and mechanism boundaries; excludes crystal solution and MOF porosity or topology."
---

## When to use

Use after a materials experiment, literature synthesis, model, or screening
study proposes that composition or processing changed structure and property.

## Do not use when

Do not infer causality from a database correlation or one before/after sample.
Use crystallography Skills for refinement validity and MOF Skills for framework
activation, porosity, and topology.

## Scientific question

Does the evidence support the stated effect for identified specimens and
conditions, and does it distinguish composition, processing, microstructure,
measurement artifact, and confounding explanations?

## Required inputs

Original specimen/process records; raw characterization and property data;
controls and replicates; calibration; model or statistical analysis; uncertainty;
candidate mechanisms; and the proposed claim.

## Decision procedure

1. Verify that specimens share the intended composition and differ only in
   interpretable factors.
2. Inspect raw property data and measurement geometry/conditions.
3. Establish whether characterization samples the relevant bulk, surface,
   interface, or local volume.
4. Quantify batch, specimen, and measurement variability separately.
5. Test simpler explanations such as density, thickness, phase fraction,
   texture, porosity, contamination, aging, or calibration drift.
6. Compare with physically meaningful baselines and literature under compatible
   conditions.
7. Require discriminating evidence before assigning a mechanism.

## Minimum capability probe

Trace one headline property and one structural descriptor from raw data through
calibration, processing, normalization, and uncertainty. Recompute a
representative value independently.

## Evidence to retain

Retain batch/specimen lineage, raw data, calibrations, processing parameters,
replicate-level results, exclusions, residuals, negative results, alternate
mechanisms, and claim-to-evidence links.

## Validation gates

- Report individual specimens and independent batches when generality is claimed.
- Do not treat technical repeats as independent material replicates.
- Separate local characterization from bulk property evidence.
- Effect size must exceed relevant uncertainty and protocol variation.
- Prediction or computed descriptors do not replace measured validation.
- Mechanistic wording requires evidence that discriminates alternatives.

## Uncertainty and applicability domain

State material family, composition, process window, specimen form, environmental
and measurement conditions, sample count, heterogeneity, and extrapolation.

## Output contract

Return a verdict of supported, narrowed, or unsupported; list direct
observations, inferred structure-property relationships, alternative causes,
uncertainty, negative results, and the maximum defensible claim.

## Stop, block, or replan conditions

Replan when sample lineage is broken, controls or independent specimens are
absent, characterization and property samples are not comparable, or the
claimed effect is within uncertainty.
