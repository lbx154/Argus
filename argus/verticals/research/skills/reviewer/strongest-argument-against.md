# The strongest argument against the paper

Use this only inside the final Review scientific pass. Read the current paper
and identify the single strongest venue-level rejection argument that survives
the obvious rebuttal.

Return, in the current Reviewer response:

1. the decisive objection;
2. the exact paper section, figure, table, or result supporting it;
3. the strongest available defense;
4. the remaining defect that would justify rejection at the venue, and the direct repair.

Do not create separate files for the objection and response, a separate stage,
or another review cycle.
The Engineer repairs the issue together with the visual and language findings;
the integrated Reviewer then reaches a judgment on whether the repaired paper holds.

## In an Idea route review

When the Idea playbook sends you here, state the one strongest objection to the
route as written and its best defense in a few sentences; do not redo the
literature search. The objection sets the route aside only when the same
mechanism and claim are already published, the mechanism is contradicted by
evidence, or the decisive experiment needs resources not here. Anything else,
including partial overlap with prior work, is a known risk: record it for
Experiment and let the route pass.
