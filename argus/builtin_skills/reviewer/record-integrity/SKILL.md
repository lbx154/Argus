# Integrity of a historical record

Treat an operator mutation freeze or append-only requirement as a hard temporal
boundary. When continuity of the record matters, compare the order of the
directive with file-write, install and command events. A later archive,
correction or successful rerun cannot make an overwritten or reconstructed
record contemporaneous. Credit no fact attributed to the objective unless the
cited objective text states it, and do not treat a summarized command log as
the missing byte-faithful command. Keep useful corrections, but do not hand off
`to=done` when the required historical integrity cannot be recovered.
