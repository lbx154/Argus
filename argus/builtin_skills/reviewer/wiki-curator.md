# Semantic Wiki Review

The Wiki is shared declarative knowledge, not a task journal or procedure library.

## Review contract

1. Read the Wiki root and `INDEX.md` yourself.
2. Inspect the edited semantic pages and relevant project evidence.
3. Require exactly `title` and `description` frontmatter plus Markdown content.
4. Reject opaque IDs, generated suffixes, metadata accumulation, and synonym pages.
5. Prefer refining the existing semantic path when it already represents the same
   knowledge.
6. Keep uncertainty and contradictory evidence explicit in ordinary prose.
7. Ensure `INDEX.md` gives a concise path and description for progressive
   disclosure.
8. Do not copy task status, handoffs, evaluator results, counters, or procedures.
9. Do not manufacture a Wiki edit merely because a mission occurred.

## Optional Insight review

A separate `## Insight` section is allowed, never required. Its absence is not a
review failure. Prefer omission to an empty section, a speculative restatement
of the summary, or an unsupported universal claim.

When present, verify that it explicitly distinguishes interpretation from
established facts. Check the linked observations behind the abstraction, its
scope, proposed transfer, and uncertainty where relevant; these are quality
guides, not a mandatory field checklist. Multiple separately useful insights may
use subheadings. A novel or provisional hypothesis can remain when its limited
evidence and possible counterexamples are clear.

Read cited Wiki pages or project evidence before accepting a cross-project
connection. Make sure descriptions and INDEX entries do not promote a hypothesis
to a fact. Keep useful `#insight` links discoverable through ordinary Markdown
and page-body search. When new evidence narrows or refutes an interpretation,
revise or explicitly withdraw it with that evidence, without discarding still-valid
factual content.

All roles may maintain the Wiki directly. The file edit, not a structured page
operation or post-mission merge, is the durable change.
