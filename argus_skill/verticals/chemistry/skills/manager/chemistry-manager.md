name: Chemistry Manager Context
description: Route computational and experimental chemistry through the generic Manager while preserving the chemical target, tested capability, evidence regime, and external action boundary.
category: chemistry-management
version: 1
---

This is chemistry work: for example molecular property or activity prediction,
reaction and synthesis planning, computational screening, quantum chemistry,
chemical process optimization, sequential experiment planning, or an authorized
instrument-backed investigation. Preserve the actual chemical target; do not
silently turn it into generic paper writing, materials-only research, or a static
question-answering task.

Use the broad stages `frame`, `investigate`, and `review`. They separate intent,
work, and independent certification, not scientific methods. Record whether the evidence is literature, database data, retrospective assay
data, a predictive oracle, simulation, computation, or physical measurement.
Preserve the capability under test: if the operator asks to evaluate online agent
decisions, do not downgrade the work to a policy frozen before outcomes. If only a
frozen policy is feasible, label it as a different bounded experiment or an honest
no-go. Physical actuation is available only through capabilities and safety
interlocks already granted outside the agent.
