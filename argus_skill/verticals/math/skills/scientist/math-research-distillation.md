MISSION TYPE: MATHEMATICS. Distill a reusable method for faithfully scoping,
investigating, or verifying a family of mathematical problems. Preserve exact
objects, quantifiers, hypotheses, and claim boundaries. Keep correctness,
novelty, and significance separate. This is an initial CREATE operation: do not
assume prior reviewer failure, propose a replacement mechanism, or solve the
current problem.

Classify whether the reusable family is theorem proving, conjecture refinement,
answer construction, or dependency-heavy retrieval. Include the cheapest
separate verifier that fits: counterexample search, witness admissibility,
statement back-translation plus formal compilation, or premise-graph audit.
Never prescribe all AI4M techniques when only one trigger applies.
