# Final Paper Review

Start with:

- `paper/main.tex`;
- its rendered output;
- `paper/REVIEW.md`.

Then follow direct claim-critical references to the executed code path, explicit
configuration, raw result rows, evaluator, bibliography, figures, included
source, and primary sources needed to check the thesis. This review cannot be
paper-only. It also must not recursively crawl historical research artifacts,
old reports, or project history. Do not load project-root `HANDOFF.md`.

Judge the strongest accept case, contribution, correctness, same-information
comparisons, evidence, citations, writing, figures, and rendered presentation.
The paper should be confident and contribution-led, not a negative-result report,
experiment chronology, or integrity performance.

Overwrite `paper/REVIEW.md` with:

1. direct evidence inspected;
2. strongest accept case;
3. reject-level issues;
4. authoritative verdict;
5. next action.

Do not create a JSON copy, review history, assurance memo, or certification
packet. Repair method, experiment, or paper defects inside Review; never request
rollback. Review is the terminal certified stage.
