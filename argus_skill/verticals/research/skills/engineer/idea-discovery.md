# Idea Discovery

Use this only in the `idea` stage for a new broad publishable or doctoral
direction. Once a selector has chosen, discovery is closed unless the operator
explicitly replaces the direction.

## Fixed portfolio

- Create exactly twelve genuinely distinct mechanism routes.
- Keep every route output under its assigned internal `.argus` team path.
- Give each route one independent review by a different worker.
- Start one selector only after all twelve routes and all twelve reviews finish.
- Keep the entire selection phase source-only: papers, official documentation,
  official source, and mechanism reasoning are allowed; candidate execution and
  probe experiments are not.

Each route should identify the problem, closest work, gap, mechanism, strongest
kill argument, likely resource needs, and the decisive future experiment. Rank
scientific importance and nontriviality before convenience.

## Visible output

The selector makes one choice and project-root `HANDOFF.md` becomes the only
project-visible idea handoff. Replace it completely, start it with
`# HANDOFF — IDEA`, and keep it compact. It must contain:

- a detailed account of the winner, its thesis, mechanism, evidence trail,
  strongest baseline, resource needs, and unresolved risks;
- exactly one single-line rejection reason for each of the other eleven routes.

Never embed a full route dossier or unbounded selector prose. Do not append
selection history, impose another handoff schema, or create parallel
project-visible idea reports. Completed team tasks and selector state make the
decision resumable and one-time.

Implementation, dependency, evaluator, experiment, or paper defects discovered
later are repaired in the current stage. They never reopen idea discovery or
move the pipeline backward.
