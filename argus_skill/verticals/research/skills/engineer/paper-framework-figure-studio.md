# Paper Framework Figure Studio

Use this in Paper for Figure 1 or another conceptual, method, architecture, or
taxonomy figure. Read `HANDOFF.md`, the current manuscript, the executed method,
and direct result sources. Create only the editable figure source and the final
export included by the paper.

## Design

1. State the figure's one-sentence scientific takeaway.
2. List the exact modules, labels, and connections, including each connection's
   source, target, direction, boundary port, and meaning.
3. Choose one clear reading direction and a compact layout that exposes the
   contribution's internal mechanism rather than hiding it in an empty box.
4. Build with editable native objects through PPT Master by default. Use
   FigureSpec, Draw.io, Graphviz, or browser SVG when exact topology is clearer.
5. Export a vector paper asset and embed it with a substantive caption and body
   reference.

## Geometry and typography

- connectors terminate at explicit node boundaries;
- no shaft or arrowhead enters an unrelated node, label, or panel;
- avoid crossings, overlaps, clipping, ambiguous feedback paths, and dead space;
- use conventional process, decision, grouping, comparison, and feedback shapes;
- use a white canvas, restrained color-blind-safe accents, and consistent type;
- labels remain readable at the actual single- or double-column size;
- every visible name, direction, and value matches the paper and executed method.

Paper needs a complete, credible figure and a successful compile. Do not create
layout reports, exemplar collections, provenance records, or visual-review
files. Strict page-by-page visual acceptance happens once, in Review.
