# Ideation patterns — vendored evidence tier

Corpus-derived research-ideation vocabulary used by the paper-ideation stage:
**15 ideation patterns** (`ideation-patterns/`) and their **31 fine-grained
sub-patterns** (`ideation-sub-patterns/C00.md … C30.md`), plus `anti-patterns.md`
(reject-enriched compositions) and `companion-combos.md` (which patterns pair
into one paper).

These are read progressively by the ideation skills:
- **Research Idea Playbook — pattern selection**: read `ideation-patterns/overview.md`
  to pick the 1–3 patterns whose operational signature closes the diagnosed gap.
- **Research Idea Playbook — instantiation**: read the ONE selected
  `ideation-sub-patterns/C##.md` tactical card (its Step-by-Step + failure mode)
  to write the candidate.
- **`novelty-check.md` / reviewer `kill-argument.md` — audit**: read the
  sub-pattern failure-mode panels and `anti-patterns.md` to check the candidate
  clears the cluster's documented reject lessons.

The pattern is diagnostic vocabulary, never the contribution claim itself.

## Provenance

Vendored verbatim from **microsoft/ResearchStudio** (MIT License), path
`ResearchStudio-Idea/skills/idea_spark/references/`. Induced in the paper

papers (incl. Oral, high-cited, and rejected).
