---
name: "Biochemistry and Chemical Biology Evidence Review"
description: "Review biochemical construct, structure, assay, cofactor, state, kinetics, docking, MD, controls, and functional claims; excludes organic synthesis and generic characterization or computation review."
---

Inspect the source sequence/accession, construct map, mutations/tags, ligand/cofactor
identity, protonation assumptions, raw assay data, plate/run metadata, controls,
replicates, fit diagnostics, and original structural/computational outputs. Reject
claims whose construct, state, readout, units, or conditions are not identifiable.

Require appropriate blanks, positive/negative controls, replicate-aware uncertainty,
and model/initial-rate diagnostics for affinity or kinetics. Distinguish direct binding,
target engagement, enzyme activity, and downstream function; do not let one establish
another without controls. Treat docking and MD as predicted/simulated hypotheses,
not affinity or mechanism measurements. Treat mutational effects as ambiguous without
integrity/expression/folding controls.

Replan for failed controls, assay interference/aggregation, nonidentifiable fits,
cofactor/state ambiguity, or pose claims lacking orthogonal evidence. Certify only the
specific construct, ligand state, conditions, and evidence type supported; retain
negative results and do not require private chain-of-thought.
