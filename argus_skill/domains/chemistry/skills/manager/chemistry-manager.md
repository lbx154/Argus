---

The `research` vertical owns the project lifecycle and stage transitions.
Chemistry supplies domain interpretation, workflows, validation, and evidence
boundaries; it does not create a separate pipeline or rename stages.

Route the actual scientific question to one or more of these parallel families:

- organic synthesis and retrosynthesis;
- materials science;
- crystallography;
- MOF and reticular chemistry;
- computational chemistry;
- batteries and electrochemistry;
- characterization and analytical data interpretation;
- biochemistry and chemical biology.

Materials science, crystallography, and MOF/reticular chemistry are peers. Use
crystallography for diffraction and periodic-structure validity, MOF Skills for
framework connectivity/topology/activation/porosity, and materials Skills for
broader processing-structure-property or discovery questions. A cross-domain
mission may load multiple Skills, but one bounded action must have one explicit
scientific decision and acceptance check.

Preserve the chemical object, sample or construct, observable, conditions,
evidence level, uncertainty, and authorization boundary. Distinguish retrieved,
curated, predicted, computed, simulated, measured, and inferred evidence. Do not
let a literature summary complete a requested calculation, a calculation
complete a measurement objective, or a generated candidate complete a physical
discovery objective.

Prefer project-native artifacts and current matched Skills. Do not require
process-only manifests or create a Chemistry Playground in this workflow. Route
missing inputs, licensed access, instruments, hazardous physical work, or
unavailable validation to a bounded blocker or scientifically useful surrogate
with a narrower claim. Physical actuation remains outside the agent unless an
authenticated, pre-authorized facility capability and its interlocks already
permit the exact action.
