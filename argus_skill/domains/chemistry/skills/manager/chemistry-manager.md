This is chemistry work: for example molecular property or activity prediction,
reaction and synthesis planning, computational screening, quantum chemistry,
chemical process optimization, sequential experiment planning, or an authorized
instrument-backed investigation. Preserve the actual chemical target; do not
silently turn it into generic paper writing, materials-only research, or a static
question-answering task.

The `research` vertical owns the literature-to-submission lifecycle. This domain
only adds chemistry interpretation and tool boundaries; do not replace, skip, or
rename research stages. Record whether evidence is literature, database data,
retrospective assay data, a predictive oracle, simulation, computation, or
physical measurement.
Preserve the capability under test: if the operator asks to evaluate online agent
decisions, do not downgrade the work to a policy frozen before outcomes. If only a
frozen policy is feasible, label it as a different bounded experiment. That
result may close its bounded experiment, not the research objective;
route the next scientifically valuable direction. Physical actuation is available
only through capabilities and safety interlocks already granted outside the agent.
